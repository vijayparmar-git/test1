AI-Assisted Security Operations

AI-assisted correlation and automation help analysts prioritize signals, investigate suspicious activity and accelerate response.

Tech and Support uses AI/ML-enabled security capabilities provided by leading third-party security platforms and technology providers, including Microsoft, Cisco, and other security technology providers.

These AI-assisted capabilities help support security monitoring, alert analysis, threat detection, investigation, prioritization, and incident response activities.

Our SOC engineers use these platform capabilities as part of the overall security operations process. AI/ML tools assist our security operations; they do not replace human security expertise. Human engineers review and respond to relevant security events in accordance with the customer's subscribed SOC service and agreed SLA.

The specific AI/ML capabilities, security tools, monitoring scope, and response processes may vary depending on the customer's technology environment and selected SOC service.
